The premise

The method came first.

Nearly a decade ago, we designed a workflow connecting research foundations to collection, structure, preservation, analysis, and reproducibility.

Large language models now provide the processing capacity that was missing. They extend the workflow; they do not replace scholarly judgment.

Editorial policy

What “living” means

Different parts of the book change at different speeds. Every public page identifies its place in the current edition; material that may drift is dated and reviewed.

01

Core method

Epistemological commitments, ethics, provenance, and evidence evaluation change slowly through reviewed releases.

02

Workflows and resources

Prompts, templates, examples, and teaching materials improve after testing, feedback, and documented pilots.

03

Tool guidance

AI systems, interfaces, prices, and privacy terms receive dates and explicit warnings when they may be stale.

Open review

Help test the method

Report a point of confusion, test a workflow, suggest a missing perspective, or describe the artifact your research produced. Feedback is reviewed editorially and documented in later versions.
